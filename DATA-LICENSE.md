Changes by CosmosEngine: selected 55 nearby primary entries; omitted companion entries; converted J2000 right ascension/declination and catalog distance into renderer ecliptic coordinates; added display aliases, spectral color estimates and estimated mass/radius/bolometric luminosity. The selection is a catalog snapshot, not a complete census or current precision astrometric solution. Stellar proper motion and binary orbital motion are not simulated.
