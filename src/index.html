Fly close to any world to reveal detailed terrain automatically. Materials follow the globe map at your latitude and longitude. Land descends beneath your current position; WASD moves and drag looks around. Shift + scroll adjusts the telescope from 0.5× to 1000×; click its indicator to reset. O returns to orbit. Gas and ice giants have no landable surface.

Planet orbits use approximate JPL elements for 1800–2050. Moon and asteroid trajectories use simplified mean elements; satellite phases and surface orientations are illustrative. This is an exploration simulator, not a precision ephemeris.
